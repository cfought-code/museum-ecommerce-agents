# Intelligence Agents

The Intelligence domain focuses on gathering, analyzing, and interpreting information to support strategic decision-making for museum retail and ecommerce operations.

## Purpose

These agents identify trends, opportunities, risks, and competitive insights that can inform merchandising, marketing, and operational decisions.

## Current Agents

### Market Research

Researches:

- Industry trends
- Consumer preferences
- Product opportunities
- Emerging artists and makers

### Analytics

Analyzes:

- Product performance
- Sales trends
- Inventory insights
- Category performance
- Operational metrics

## Planned Agents

### Competitor Intelligence

Tracks:

- Competitor assortments
- Featured products
- Pricing strategies
- Promotional activity

### Pricing Intelligence

Monitors:

- Price positioning
- Competitive pricing
- Margin opportunities

### Trend Intelligence

Identifies:

- Seasonal opportunities
- Emerging design trends
- Gift-giving patterns

## Outputs

- Market research reports
- Competitor reports
- Performance dashboards
- Executive recommendations
- Product opportunity analyses
