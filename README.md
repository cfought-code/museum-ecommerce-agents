# Museum Ecommerce Agents

AI-powered workflows for ecommerce analytics, SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), market research, and content generation.

## Overview

This repository provides a framework for building agentic workflows that help ecommerce teams make better decisions through automation and AI-assisted analysis.

## Planned Capabilities

### Ecommerce Analytics

Analyze:

- Sales performance
- Product performance
- Revenue trends
- Inventory movement
- Customer purchasing behavior

### SEO Auditing

Review:

- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Heading structure
- Internal links
- Content quality

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Optimize content for:

- Microsoft Copilot
- ChatGPT
- Google AI Overviews
- Perplexity
- Voice assistants

### Market Research

Track:

- Industry trends
- Competitor activity
- Search demand
- Product opportunities

### Content Generation

Generate:

- Product descriptions
- FAQ content
- Collection pages
- Buying guides
- Artist biographies
